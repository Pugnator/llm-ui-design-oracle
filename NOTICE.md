# Notice — third-party material

This repository is public. What follows is a precise account of what in it is
original and what is not, so the position is legible rather than assumed.

## Original

The rules, their rationales, review tests, the conflict analyses, the
anti-pattern catalogue, the checklist, the contract, the modules, and
**Appendix A of `UI_ORACLE.md`** are original text written for this
repository.

The figures in `img/` are generated by `tools/make_figures.py` in this
repository. No third-party images, icons or fonts are included or hot-linked.

## Archived source text (`corpus/`)

Every web page a rule quotes is archived as text so that a citation can be
checked against what was read. Each file's header records its URL, the date
it was fetched and its licence. Page chrome (navigation, feedback widgets)
was removed; the guidance text is unaltered.

dialog semantics: *Design principles*, *Design guidelines overview*, *Color in
Windows*, *Typography in Windows*, *Accessible text requirements*,
*Commanding basics*, and the Win32 UX Guide's *Dialog Boxes*. Documentation

also CC BY 4.0), attributed by URL, each header carrying the page's own
`ms.date`. Numeric specifications are quoted **exactly** in the rules, because
paraphrasing a specification destroys it.

## Books

Four books inform the interaction-design rules:

- Alan Cooper, Robert Reimann, David Cronin, Christopher Noessel — *About
  Face: The Essentials of Interaction Design*, 4th ed.
- Jenifer Tidwell, Charles Brewer, Aynne Valencia — *Designing Interfaces*,
  3rd ed.
- Jeff Johnson — *Designing with the Mind in Mind*, 3rd ed.
- Don Norman — *The Design of Everyday Things*, revised ed.

**No substantial portion of any of them is reproduced here.** What appears is:

1. **Restatement.** Appendix A summarises what each book contributes, in this
   repository's own words.
2. **Short quotations for commentary.** Single sentences or phrases, each
   attributed to author, work and chapter, retained only where the exact
   wording carries the distinction being drawn — for example Norman's
   separation of affordances from signifiers, where a paraphrase blurs the
   very point.

The books are **not** included in this repository (the `docs/` directory
that holds them locally is ignored by git) and are not required to use it;
that is what Appendix A is for.
